Context

Enterprise digital retail and e-commerce initiative for one of the largest food and drug retailers in the United States, spanning online grocery, loyalty, pharmacy, and in-store customer experiences.

What I built

  1. 01

    Cart & checkout

    Developed cart, pricing, promotions, and digital-coupon (“clip to card”) flows as React storefront features on Node.js services, with Redis caching for cart state and store-level stock availability.

  2. 02

    Performance

    Reduced query latency by more than 66% (1,500 ms to under 500 ms) and improved MySQL and PostgreSQL performance by 30% through query optimization, indexing, and caching strategies.

  3. 03

    Experimentation & impact

    Increased conversion by 8%, user engagement by 12%, and overall revenue by 9% by shipping A/B test variants in React and analyzing results with Python ETL pipelines, and reduced production issues by 20% through stronger ETL validation, UAT, and release-readiness practices.
